🔎 What this dashboard is honest about

• 2 years of data only (Aug 2024 missing) — each month seen ~2 times.
• A market's own daily arrivals barely predict its price (r ≈ −0.03) — price is set by state/national supply.
• The ML forecaster ties a naive baseline — monthly prices are near a random walk; its value is the uncertainty band.
• The profit calculator uses assumed transport & commission the farmer can adjust — a net estimate, not a guarantee.
• No weather / policy data — so no model here can foresee a price shock.

Methodology: 31,930 daily Agmarknet onion records (111 markets, 14 curated) → cleaned → seasonal & market analysis (OLS, HAC robust SEs) + a monthly per-mandi forecaster (persistence + LightGBM ensemble with quantile intervals). Full pipeline reproducible with one command.
Disclaimer: Figures are estimates for advisory use, not financial guarantees. Prices are gross; the calculator's transport, labour and commission are user-adjustable assumptions. In supply-shock periods the real peak price will be higher than shown.
